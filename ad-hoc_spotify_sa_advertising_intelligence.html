Strategic Media Notes

Podcast Power

With 48% growth in video podcasts, South Africans are increasingly leaning into long-form audio-visual storytelling. This is the prime spot for 'Host-read' sponsorships.

High Intent

Local Language Growth

Royalties for isiZulu (+112%) and Sotho (+345%) are booming. Ad creative produced in vernacular languages outperforms generic English ads by 2.4x in SA.

Cultural Resonance

The "Free" Opportunity

While Premium is growing, ~60% of the SA user base is on the Ad-Supported tier. This represents the "Mass Market" middle class with high mobile data consumption.

The Advertiser's Cheat Sheet

Best For:

  • FMCG brands targeting the youth & middle class
  • Tech & Telecom service providers
  • Financial services (The avg. age is 31—prime for banking/insurance)

Avoid:

  • Static audio only (Leverage the new 'Video' supply)
