Post-Campaign Action Items & Diagnostics

1 Reduce Landing Page Weight

Mobile users in South Africa on mobile connections drop off rapidly if a page exceeds a 2-second load time. Compress images, delay heavy JS scripts, and optimize styling dependencies to capture clicked traffic.

2 Frictionless Conversions

An average duration of just 5 seconds indicates users are bouncing almost immediately. Simplify landing page layouts, position your form above the fold, and cut down requirements to 2 or 3 essential inputs.

3 Migrate to Meta Instant Forms

With 99.1% of traffic arriving on mobile, external hops are costly. Use Meta's On-Platform Native Lead Forms to bypass load latency entirely, auto-filling profile fields to immediately drive down acquisition costs.
