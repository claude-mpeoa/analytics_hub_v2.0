Post-Campaign Action Items & Diagnostics

1 Reduce Landing Page Weight

Mobile users in South Africa on standard cell connections drop off rapidly if a page exceeds a 2-second loading threshold. Optimise image sizes, eliminate heavy JS, and minify styling packages on your landing page.

2 Optimise Form Layout

An 10s average duration means users read the page but bail on the form. Keep form fields minimal (3 fields max: Name, Phone/WhatsApp, Email). Add frictionless options like auto-fill or social signup.

3 Leverage Instant Forms

For rapid mobile acquisition campaigns, bypass external hosting entirely. Run Meta's Native Lead Forms (Instant Forms). It guarantees 100% click-to-lead delivery and pre-fills user contact info.
