South African Market Trends

1. The "Vitality" Lock-in Effect

Discovery Vitality members are incentivized to use Virgin Active (VA). However, VA's generic model lacks the Diastasis Recti and Pelvic Health clinical depth now demanded by the top-tier "Fourth Trimester" demographic.

2. Clinical De-medicalization

Pelvic Health is moving from "Physio rooms" into "Studio environments." In SA, boutiques like The Movement Lab are winning because they feel like a luxury spa but act like a clinic.

3. The Childcare Friction Point

Despite high demand for clinical care, boutique churn is high because they lack childcare. Virgin Active Point wins on "Convenience," but loses on "Outcome."
