Marketing Metric Formulas & Interpretation

Conversion Rate (CVR)
(Sales / Leads) × 100

Measures the efficiency of your lead nurturing pipeline. A positive trend means leads are of higher quality or sales processes are closing more effectively.

Cost Per Lead (CPL)
Total Spend / Total Leads

Evaluates customer acquisition efficiency. Lower CPL indicates successful campaign ad targeting adjustments or lower bid costs on South African target ad exchange networks.

Cost Per Sale (CPS)
Total Spend / Total Sales

Calculates final acquisition cost in Rands. This metric is directly linked to your profit margins. Ideally, your average deal value should far exceed CPS.
